Moving Checklist

Moving Tips from Our Crew

A few simple habits that make moving day smoother.

01

Book early

Fix your date a few days ahead, and earlier for long-distance moves or month-end dates.

02

Declutter first

Donate or discard what you no longer use. Less to move means a lighter, cheaper move.

03

Label every box

Write the room and contents on each box so unpacking is quick and nothing goes missing.

04

Pack an essentials bag

Keep chargers, medicines, toiletries and a change of clothes with you for the first night.

05

Photograph the setup

Take a quick photo of TV, router and appliance cables before disconnecting them.

06

Keep valuables with you

Carry cash, jewellery and important documents yourself instead of packing them.

What Affects Your Moving Cost

Every move is different. Here is what we look at when preparing your estimate.

  • Distance

    Local shifting within Kadapa costs less than an interstate move.

  • Volume of goods

    The more you move, the bigger the vehicle and the larger the crew we need.

  • Floor & lift access

    Higher floors without a lift take more time and manpower.

  • Packing materials

    Fragile and high-value items need extra layers of protection.

  • Vehicle type

    Shared or dedicated space, and the size of the vehicle that fits your move.

  • Special items

    Heavy appliances, vehicles or bulky furniture may need extra handling.
