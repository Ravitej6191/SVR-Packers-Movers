House Shifting Checklist

Your House Shifting Checklist

Simple steps we share with every family, from booking to settling in.

01

2 weeks before

  • Fix your date (and muhurat, if you follow one) and book your packers
  • Donate or sell things you no longer use
  • Take permission from society office or landlord for lift and parking
  • Plan address change for Aadhaar, bank, LPG, electricity and water
  • Keep original documents and valuables in one bag
02

1 week before

  • Start packing items you rarely use
  • Label every box room-wise
  • Take photos of TV, set-top box and wiring
  • Book a technician to uninstall AC and washing machine
  • Empty and defrost the fridge a day before
03

Shifting day

  • Keep cash, jewellery and documents with you
  • Pack an essentials bag: medicines, chargers, clothes
  • Check each room with the crew before loading
  • Carry idols and pooja items yourself, and keep water and snacks ready
  • Check cupboards, balcony and terrace one last time
04

After the move

  • Check all items against your list
  • Get AC, TV and Wi-Fi or DTH set up again
  • Unpack kitchen and pooja essentials first
  • Shift LPG, electricity and water connections
  • Tell us at once if anything is damaged

Transparent Estimates

What Affects Your Shifting Cost

Every move is different. Here is what we look at when preparing your estimate.

  • Distance

    Local shifting within Kadapa costs less than an interstate move.

  • Volume of goods

    The more you move, the bigger the vehicle and the larger the crew we need.

  • Floor & lift access

    Higher floors without a lift take more time and manpower.

  • Packing materials

    Fragile and high-value items need extra layers of protection.

  • Vehicle type

    Part load or full load, and the size of the vehicle that fits your shifting.

  • Special items

    Heavy appliances, vehicles or bulky furniture may need extra handling.
