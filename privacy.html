Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This notice explains what personal data SVR Packers & Movers ("we", "us") collects when you use this website or contact us, why we need it, and the choices and rights you have. We keep it simple: this website has no accounts, does not store your quote details on a server, and we do not sell your information.

2. What personal data we handle

  • Details you share to get a quote or book a move: your name, phone number, pick-up and drop locations, type of service, preferred date, and anything you write in the details box, such as the items to be moved.
  • Records of calls, WhatsApp messages and emails you exchange with us.
  • Basic visit statistics about how the website is used, collected in aggregate through Vercel Web Analytics. We do not use this to identify you.

3. Why we use it

  • To prepare your quote and plan your move.
  • To carry out the move, and to contact you about pick-up, delivery and any changes.
  • To keep business, tax and accounting records that the law requires.
  • To understand how many people visit the website, so we can improve it.

We use your data only for these purposes.

4. How the quote form works

When you fill in the "Request a Quote" form, the website does not send your details to a server. It opens WhatsApp with a message already written from what you typed. Nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is provided by Meta and handles messages under its own privacy policy.

5. Your consent, and withdrawing it

You choose what to share with us, and by sending us your details you agree that we may use them for the purposes above. You can withdraw that consent at any time by telling us on the phone number or email above. If you withdraw it, we will stop using your details for your enquiry, except where we must keep records by law or to finish a move already booked.

6. Cookies and third-party services

  • We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and the website's fonts and images are served from the site itself.
  • Google Maps. The contact section and some pages show an embedded Google map. When it loads, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this website and provides the aggregate analytics mentioned above.
  • Links to other websites, such as the GST portal, are governed by those websites' own policies.

7. Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to complete your move, such as our packing crew and drivers, and authorities where the law requires. We do not sell your data or share it with others for their own marketing.

8. How we protect it

We keep your details to the people who need them and take reasonable steps to prevent loss or misuse. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information such as bank passwords.

9. How long we keep it

We keep move-related details for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

10. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold and how we use it;
  • correct or complete inaccurate data;
  • erase your data, unless we need to keep it by law;
  • address a grievance about how we handle your data; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you are unable to.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

11. If you are not satisfied

Please contact us first so we can try to resolve the matter. If you are still not satisfied, you can make a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India, as provided in the Act.

12. Children

This website is for adults arranging a move. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children. If you think a child has shared data with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
