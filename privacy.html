Information you give us

  • Quote form. When you fill in the "Request a Quote" form (name, phone number, from and to locations, type of service, preferred date, and any details), the website does not send it to a server. How we use it

  • To prepare your quote, plan the pick-up and delivery, and carry out your move.
  • To answer your questions and keep you updated about your move.
  • To keep business, tax and accounting records as the law requires.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to complete your move, such as our packing crew and drivers, and where the law requires it. We do not share your details with third parties for their own marketing.

Website visits and third-party services

  • Vercel Web Analytics gives us overall visit numbers (for example, how many people viewed a page). We use it to improve the site, not to identify you personally.

  • WhatsApp is provided by Meta. Messages you send are handled under WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • The website's fonts and images are served from this site itself. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep move-related details for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, and then delete or anonymise them.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it (unless we must keep it by law). Contact us using the details above and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children
