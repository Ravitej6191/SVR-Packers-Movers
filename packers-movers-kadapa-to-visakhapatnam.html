Tips for moving to Visakhapatnam

A few things worth knowing before you fix your date.

  • Vizag is humid and close to the sea. Wrapped furniture and well-sealed cartons help keep moisture away from wood, mattresses and electronics.
  • Because it is a long route, book at least 5–7 days ahead, as we recommend for all long-distance moves.
  • Tell us if the new place is on a hill or a narrow lane, so we can plan how the vehicle reaches the door.

How much does it cost to move from Kadapa to Visakhapatnam?

The cost depends on how much you are moving, the floor and lift access at both ends, the packing materials needed and the type of vehicle. Share your details on WhatsApp and we will reply with a clear estimate before you decide.

How long does a Kadapa to Visakhapatnam move take?

It depends on the distance of about 750 km, the size of the load, and road and weather conditions. We tell you the expected delivery date when your booking is confirmed.

Do I need permission from my society or building for the move?

Many apartment communities in Visakhapatnam ask for prior intimation or a booking for the lift and parking. Please check with your society or landlord at both ends and tell us the timings, so we can plan around them.

Can you also move my car or bike from Kadapa to Visakhapatnam?

Yes. We handle car and bike transport along with household goods. Mention it when you ask for a quote.
