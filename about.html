Who we are

SVR Packers & Movers is a packers and movers company based in Y.S. Nagar, Kadapa. We handle house shifting, office shifting, car and bike transport, packing and unpacking, loading and unloading, and short-term storage, within Kadapa, across Andhra Pradesh and to any city in India.
