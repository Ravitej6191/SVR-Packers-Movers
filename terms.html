Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 12 October 2026

These terms apply when you book a move or any other service with SVR Packers & Movers ("we", "us"), and when you use this website. Please read them together with your quotation. If your written quotation says something different, the quotation applies.

1. Quotations and booking

  • We prepare a quotation from the details you give us: items, addresses, floors, lift access, distance and date. Please be accurate. If the actual load or conditions are different, the price may change, and we will tell you before we proceed.
  • A booking is confirmed when we confirm the date with you, and any advance agreed in the quotation is paid.

2. Payment

  • Payment terms, including any advance and the balance due on delivery, are stated in your quotation.
  • Please ask for a receipt or bill for every payment. Our GSTIN is 37DURPR1470K1ZV.

3. Packing, loading and transport

  • We pack with materials such as bubble wrap, stretch film, corrugated sheets and cartons, and load your goods carefully.
  • Please be present, or have someone you trust present, to check the items as they are packed and loaded.
  • Goods are transported on our vehicles, which carry an All India Permit for moves between states.

4. Items we cannot carry

For safety and legal reasons we cannot carry perishable food, flammable or hazardous goods such as gas cylinders, chemicals and fuel, illegal items, or cash, jewellery and important documents. Please keep these with you. If you want us to carry any unusual or very valuable item, tell us in advance.

5. Access, permissions and your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for arranging permission from your society, building or landlord for the move, including lift and parking, at both addresses.
  • Please make sure our crew and vehicle can reach the premises at the agreed time. Delays, extra waiting or extra carrying caused by access problems may attract additional charges.
  • Disconnecting and reconnecting appliances such as air conditioners needs a technician, which you should arrange unless we have agreed otherwise in writing.

6. Delivery

We give an expected delivery date when your booking is confirmed. Delivery times depend on distance, road and weather conditions, permits and other factors, so they are estimates. We will keep you informed if there is a delay.

7. Cancellation and rescheduling

Please tell us as early as possible if you need to cancel or change your date. Any charges for cancellation or rescheduling, and whether an advance is refundable, are as stated in your quotation or agreed with you in writing.

8. Damage, loss and claims

  • Please check your goods when they are delivered, and tell us about any damage or missing items as soon as possible, ideally the same day, with photographs.
  • Please do not discard damaged packing or items until we have had the chance to inspect them.
  • We will look into every claim fairly. Our responsibility is limited as set out in your quotation or agreement, and any goods-in-transit insurance applies only if it has been arranged and the policy terms are met.

9. Events beyond our control

We are not responsible for delay or failure caused by things outside our reasonable control, such as natural disasters, floods, strikes, road closures, accidents on the route, or government orders.

  • We try to keep the website accurate, but it is provided as general information and is not a binding quotation.
  • We are not responsible for the content of other websites we link to.
